Our Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how AutoFlow collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and services.

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, business name, and website URL when you submit our contact forms or use our free scan tool.

We use your information to respond to your inquiries, provide the services you request, and send you relevant communications about our services. We do not sell your information to third parties.

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored securely and only accessible to authorised personnel.

Contact Us
